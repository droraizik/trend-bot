    Copy Berkshire

    Every three months, Berkshire Hathaway must publish the US stocks it owns in a public report to the SEC, called a 13F. It comes out up to 45 days after the quarter ends.

    This line buys the same stocks in the same proportions on the day each report comes out, and holds them until the next one. It can’t copy Berkshire’s cash pile, its private companies or its insurance business, only the stocks in the report.
